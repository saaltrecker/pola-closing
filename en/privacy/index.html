Privacy statement for Pola

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This statement describes which personal data Pola processes, for what, on which legal basis, for how long, and which service providers are involved. Pola is the matchmaking assistant of the Political Communication Summit (PCS) and is operated by Partisan GmbH.

Controller

Partisan GmbH, Hausvogteiplatz 12 (c/o Mindspace), 10117 Berlin, Germany, represented by Josef Lentsch. E-mail: contact@partisan.community, phone +49 1577 4051911. Please send privacy requests to this address.

The essentials

  • All data is processed and stored exclusively on servers in the European Union (France).
  • Your data is not used to train AI models.
  • Audio is processed only to turn it into text and is not stored.
  • Pola never approaches anyone on your behalf and does not share your data with other participants.
  • You can delete your account and all conversations yourself at any time; everything is deleted at the latest 30 days after the summit.

The processing steps

1. Account

Data: first name, last name, e-mail address, password (stored only as a hash, scrypt), language choice, time of registration. A session cookie keeps you signed in on the device.
Purpose: recognising you in the next conversation, showing your conversations, suggestions and profile on any device, and sending you the summary.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (use of the account).

2. Conversation

Data: your voice is transmitted over an encrypted connection (WebRTC) during the conversation and continuously turned into text; the audio is not stored. What is stored is the transcript (what you and Pola said) as part of your conversation.
Purpose: holding the conversation, understanding the interview, answering you.
Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without it, Pola cannot talk to you.

3. Your profile

Data: what Pola notes from the conversation for your profile: role and organisation, what you work on, whom you want to meet, what you offer, whom Pola should not suggest, and whether others may find you. For every entry the sentence it came from is kept as evidence. The entries are stored as facts in Pola's graph database, each with source and time.
Special categories: what you say about your work may reveal political opinions (Art. 9 GDPR). Pola processes such information only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), given at registration and revocable at any time.
Purpose: finding the right people; not asking you everything again next time.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR.

4. Matching

Data: your profile is turned into numeric vectors ("embeddings") and compared with the profiles of the summit's speakers, partners and registered participants. The suggestions shown to you, the reason for each, and your reaction (meet / not a match) are stored.
Where the other profiles come from: the summit's public programme (politicalcomms.eu), Partisan's speaker and partner lists, and the ticket registrations (name, e-mail address, from which the organisation is derived). For organisations Pola reads their own website to describe them in one sentence. Individual people are not researched on the internet.
Is your data shown to others? Only if you explicitly agree in the conversation that Pola may suggest you to others, and only with name, role and organisation. Pola shares contact details only for people with a public role (speakers, partners), as Partisan knows them.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

5. Summary by e-mail

Data: the people you want to meet, with the reason, session and contact channels, sent to your account's e-mail address or an address you name. Sending is logged with address, time and status.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you requested the summary).

6. Operation, security, quality

Data: server logs with IP address, requested address and time; technical logs of the conversation (timings and the recognised turns per conversation id) to check recognition and answer quality.
Purpose: secure operation, troubleshooting, quality assurance.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, working service).

External service providers (processors)

The following providers process data on our behalf, each under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR:

  • Scaleway SAS, 8 rue de la Ville-l'Évêque, 75008 Paris, France (RCS Paris 433 115 904, privacy@scaleway.com).
    Hosting: the whole service, including the database with accounts, conversations, profiles and suggestions, runs on a Scaleway server in Paris (region fr-par). The audio connection goes straight there too, or through our own relay on the same server when a network blocks the direct path.
    AI language models (Scaleway Generative APIs, Paris region): Pola's replies in the conversation (model Mistral Medium 3.5), noting your details and judging the suggestions (Mistral Small 3.2), and the embeddings for matching (Qwen3-Embedding). The conversation texts and profiles are transmitted; the requests serve only to produce the answer and are not used for training.
    E-mail (Scaleway Transactional Email, Paris): the summary to your address.
  • Gradium SAS, 151bis rue Saint-Honoré, 75001 Paris, France (RCS Paris 989 284 955, dpo@gradium.ai).
    Speech recognition and speech output via the EU endpoint (eu.api.gradium.ai): your audio is turned into text and Pola's text into speech. "Zero Data Retention" is enabled for our account: inputs and outputs are deleted immediately after processing and are not used for training.

Without processing of your personal data: the service's TLS certificate comes from Let's Encrypt (Internet Security Research Group, USA); only the domain name is transmitted. The address pola-ai.eu is resolved through Scaleway's DNS service. No analytics tools, advertising cookies, external fonts or social networks are embedded.

Retention

  • Account, conversations, profile, suggestions, reactions and delivery logs: until you delete them, at the latest 30 days after the summit (6 November 2026).
  • "Delete my data" in your profile: your profile disappears from matchmaking and every view at once; the records are removed for good within 30 days.
  • Session cookie: 90 days or until you sign out. Server and conversation logs: 30 days after the summit.
  • Audio: not stored.

Cookies and local storage

One strictly necessary cookie ("pola_session", HttpOnly) keeps you signed in. Your language choice is kept in your browser's local storage. Neither requires consent (§ 25(2) TDDDG).

Security

Encrypted transport (TLS, WebRTC/DTLS-SRTP), hashed passwords, access to a conversation only with its secret key, access to account and history only with your session, servers in the EU only, regular backups in the same data centre.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Access and erasure are available directly in the app (profile, transcript, "Delete my data"); for anything else write to contact@partisan.community. We answer within one month.

Complaints: Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, datenschutz-berlin.de, or any other competent supervisory authority.

No automated decisions

Pola's suggestions are recommendations you decide on. There is no decision with legal effect or similarly significant impact within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

Changes

This statement is updated when the processing changes. The version published here applies.
